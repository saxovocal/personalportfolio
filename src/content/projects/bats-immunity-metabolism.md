## Scientific background
Bats offer a distinctive perspective on the relationship between immunity and metabolism. Their unusual physiological characteristics motivate questions about how cells coordinate responses to infection and metabolic demands. This was the focus of Wharton's doctoral research at Duke-NUS Medical School.

## Research questions
- How do immune cell populations and their transcriptional programmes vary across biological contexts?
- How might metabolic processes interact with immune responses?
- Which high-throughput approaches can help characterize these relationships?

## Methodological approach
The research draws on single-cell transcriptomics, immunological characterization, and high-throughput analytical methods. These approaches help resolve cellular heterogeneity and frame testable questions about host–pathogen interactions.
